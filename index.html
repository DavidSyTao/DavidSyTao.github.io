VLA + RL

Precision Insertion via π0.5 + RL Token for Electrical-Cabinet Inspection

  • Froze the π0.5 foundation policy and extracted an RL Token from its multimodal hidden states to drive a lightweight Actor-Critic for local action refinement (Piper, Jetson AGX Orin)
  • Focused RL on the fine alignment + insertion phase via critical-phase reset, collecting only ~60 key-segment trajectories
  • Added targeted human intervention at failure onset to build balanced success/failure and corrective samples for the critic
  • Sample-efficient off-policy RL raised the key-insertion success rate from 7.5% to 92.5% over 40 real-robot trials
VLA · Async Control · Real-World Deployment

Jitter Diagnosis & Asynchronous Continuous Control for π0.5 VLA

  • Built a six-dimensional real-robot jitter diagnosis pipeline (phenomenon → data → policy output → low-level control → chunk timing → vision/comms) to localize intra-chunk jitter vs. inter-chunk discontinuity
  • Benchmarked RTC / T-RTC / VLASH under varying delay on LeRobot / LIBERO; VLASH's future-state awareness stayed most stable as delay grew
  • Trained VLASH async with temporal delay augmentation and launched inference early (~step 42) to hide latency within a ~267ms overlap window
  • Raised task success from 5% to 65%, cut mean control period 5123.4ms → 224.5ms and task time 51s → 18s (~2.8× speedup) on Piper + Jetson AGX Orin
VLA · PEFT · Algorithm Research

π0.5 VLA Full/LoRA Fine-tuning, Adapter Capacity & Flow Sampling Study

  • Reproduced the LIBERO benchmark with a JAX Policy Server + client loop (~2000 rollouts); dual-H200 FSDP full SFT reached 96.55% average success
  • Established a low-cost LoRA baseline (~1/28–1/30 the GPU-hours of full SFT); a 30K-step LoRA main config reached 97.0% average success
  • LoRA rank ablation (PaliGemma 4/8/16/32/64): Long-Horizon was most sensitive to adapter capacity, +23.2 pct (30.0% → 53.2%)
  • Flow-matching step ablation showed success saturates by ~5 steps (1/3/5/10-step: 95.9/96.6/97.0/96.9%)
  • Manual failure analysis of 16 episodes: grasping precision dominated (8/16); remaining bottleneck is local manipulation accuracy, not sequential failure
